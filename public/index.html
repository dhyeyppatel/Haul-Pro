BEGINNER'S GUIDE & OVERVIEW

1. Upload Anything Without Limits

Click + New or drag and drop files anywhere. Upload photos, 4K videos, audio tracks, PDFs, archives, or code files. All files are preserved in original lossless quality.

2. Folders & Effortless Organization

Create folders to organize your collection. Drag and drop items into folders or click the 3-dots menu to move files between folders. Nested folders are fully supported.

3. Private by Default with Public Sharing

Every uploaded file is strictly Private by default. When you are ready to publish, click the badge or 3-dots menu to switch to Public. Public items appear on your profile.

4. Personalized Public Profile Showcase

Your unique link (e.g. shelfie.dhyey.cc/yourname) serves as your link-in-bio showcase. Customize your profile photo, display name, and bio to present your portfolio to anyone.

5. Lightning-Fast CDN Direct Links

Every file comes with a direct CDN URL powered by Telegram Cloud & Cloudflare Edge. Perfect for embedding in Discord, GitHub READMEs, websites, and forums.

APPLE-INSPIRED TRANSPARENT PRIVACY POLICY

1. Our Fundamental Privacy Commitment

At Haul Pro, privacy is not an afterthought; it is our foundation. We believe that your digital content belongs exclusively to you. We do not track your internet browsing, sell your personal data to advertisers, or monetize your activity.

2. Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers

We do not embed third-party tracking pixels, marketing beacons, or surveillance cookies. The only cookies we store are strictly essential, cryptographically signed HTTP-only session tokens (auth_token) required to keep you securely signed in.

3. Full Ownership of Your Content

You retain 100% intellectual property ownership of all images, videos, audio, documents, and code uploaded to your account. We never claim any license or ownership over your content.

4. Robust Cloud Isolation & Encryption

All data transmissions between your browser and our edge servers are protected with TLS 1.3 encryption. Private files are shielded with session authorization: unauthorized requests are rejected at the edge.

5. Absolute Right to Permanent Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

You maintain complete control over your data. When you delete a file or empty your Trash, our system dispatches a purge command to our storage engine. When you delete your account, your user record, folders, metadata, and all stored media are permanently purged from all databases and storage nodes.
